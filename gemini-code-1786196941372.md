# Personal Finance & FIRE Calculator

A responsive, glassmorphism-styled web application designed to help users take control of their personal finances, plan for early retirement (FIRE), and calculate long-term investment returns. 

Designed and developed by Austin, a Product Designer focusing on intuitive and highly reusable component systems.

## ✨ Features

- **Budget Planner**: Categorize and track your daily, monthly, and yearly expenses using the "Needs vs. Wants" framework. Automatically weights costs based on frequency.
- **FIRE Calculator (Financial Independence, Retire Early)**: Input your current age, target retirement age, and expected expenses to calculate your required FIRE number and the monthly investment needed to bridge the gap.
- **Regular Investment Calculator**: A compound interest calculator to visualize the future value of consistent monthly investments over time.
- **Dark / Light Theme**: Seamlessly switch between light and dark modes with sleek, frosted-glass (Glassmorphism) transitions.
- **Bilingual Support**: Fully localized in English (EN) and Traditional Chinese (ZH).
- **Fully Responsive**: Fluid grids and horizontal-scrolling tables ensure a perfect user experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

This project is built focusing on simplicity and zero-build configuration for easy deployment.

- **HTML5 & CSS3**
- **Vue.js 3** (via CDN) - For reactive data binding and smooth transition animations.
- **Tailwind CSS** (via CDN) - For rapid utility-first styling and glassmorphism effects (`backdrop-blur`).
